# Eigenquestions

**Job.** Find the question whose answer makes the other live questions easy or irrelevant.

**Not this.** The question is already settled and the work is a cause, a choice among known options, or a plan. Also not a check on a frame you mean to keep (map is not the territory).

**Diagram.** A short list of candidate questions, one of them marked.

**Steps.**
1. List the questions people are actually debating. Drop slogans and preferred solutions.
2. Keep only questions whose answer would change a decision or a design this cycle.
3. For each survivor, name what becomes obvious or unnecessary once it is answered.
4. Select the question that collapses the most of the rest. Write it as one sentence a reasonable person could disagree with.
5. Park the others until that answer exists. Do not solve them in this pass.

**Source.** Shishir Mehrotra, [Eigenquestions](https://docs.superhuman.com/@shishir/eigenquestions-the-art-of-framing-problems).
