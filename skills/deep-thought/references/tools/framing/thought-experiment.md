# Thought experiment

**Job.** Test a frame or a commitment by walking a concrete case through it before you build.

**Not this.** A real probe is cheap and safe. Use the scientific method and run it. Imagination is the tool when contact with the world is too expensive for this pass.

**Diagram.** The claim, then one concrete case walked to an ending.

**Steps.**
1. State the claim or plan in one sentence.
2. Keep the real constraints. Change only what the claim requires.
3. Walk one specific case through, including the person who bears the worst outcome.
4. Record what broke, which assumption did the work, and which observation would change your mind.
5. Commit, change the frame, or replace this rehearsal with a real probe. Do not treat the rehearsal as evidence from the world.

**Source.** Thought experiment as a mental model. [Farnam Street](https://fs.blog/mental-models/); James Clear's mental-model shortlist.
