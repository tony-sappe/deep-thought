# Leverage points

**Job.** Say where an intervention actually bites, using Meadows' order from weak to strong, and stop pushing a weaker point by habit.

**Not this.** You have not yet described the system (iceberg, stock and flow, or a loop). Ranking an intervention you cannot locate is numerology. The order is a guide, not a scoreboard that outranks evidence about this system.

**Diagram.** The intervention you are currently proposing, placed on the list, with one higher point you could actually reach.

Number 12 is the weakest. Number 1 is the strongest.

1. **Transcending paradigms.** The ability to hold a worldview without being captured by it.
2. **Paradigms.** The shared mindset out of which the goal and the rules fall.
3. **Goals.** What the system is for.
4. **Self-organization.** The power to add, change, or evolve structure.
5. **Rules.** Incentives, punishments, and constraints.
6. **Information flows.** Who does and does not see which information, when.
7. **Reinforcing-loop gain.** How hard a self-amplifying loop runs.
8. **Balancing-loop strength.** How hard correction can push.
9. **Delays.** How long relative to the rates of change.
10. **Stock-and-flow structure.** The physical arrangement of stocks and flows.
11. **Buffers.** The size of stabilizing stocks relative to their flows.
12. **Numbers.** Constants and parameters (quotas, tax rates, thresholds, subsidies).

**Steps.**
1. Write the intervention currently proposed, in one sentence.
2. Place it at a number. Parameters and targets are 12 unless they change a higher point.
3. Name the highest point on the list that this group can actually change in this pass. Ignore points you can only admire.
4. Say which weaker move you will stop treating as the intervention.
5. If the higher point is a goal or a paradigm, name the evidence that people will act on it. A relabeling is still a 12.

**Source.** Donella Meadows, "Leverage Points: Places to Intervene in a System," [Donella Meadows Project](https://donellameadows.org/systems-thinking-resources/). Her essay presents them from 12, the weakest, toward 1, the strongest. This page lists the same numbers starting at 1. The numbers are hers. The reading order is this page's.
