# Reinforcing loop

**Job.** Find the structure where a change produces more of the same change, up or down.

**Not this.** The structure seeks a goal (balancing loop). Two actors arming against each other, or one resource flowing to the current winner, have their own page (escalation and success to the successful). Do not flatten those into "a virtuous cycle".

**Diagram.** A loop with an even number of opposite-direction links (zero counts as even), labeled as growing or collapsing.

**Steps.**
1. Name the quantity that is growing or collapsing.
2. Name the consequence that feeds back and increases that same change.
3. Say whether the loop is running up or down. Collapse is reinforcing too.
4. Find the balancing loop or the limit that will meet it. Unbounded reinforcing stories are unfinished.
5. Do not read an exponential stretch as a straight line, and do not propose a parameter tweak as if it breaks the loop.

**Source.** Reinforcing, or positive, feedback, as used by Jay Forrester and by Donella Meadows in *Thinking in Systems*. [Untools](https://untools.co) is a worksheet. Escalation and success to the successful are separate pages.
