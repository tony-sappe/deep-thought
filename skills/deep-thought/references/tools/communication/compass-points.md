# Compass points

**Job.** Get a fast group readout on a proposal: what excites, what worries, what we need to know, and what stance we take.

**Not this.** A decision matrix or a vote dressed up as reflection. The stance comes last, after the other three have been said. East, West, North, South are labels, not geography.

**Diagram.** Four bearings. E excite. W worrisome. N need to know. S stance (suggestion or steps).

**Steps.**
1. State the proposal in one sentence.
2. East: what is useful or exciting if it works? Collect more than one voice.
3. West: what is worrisome or risky? Specifics, not "it might fail".
4. North: what do we need to know before a stance is responsible? Separate facts we can get from tastes.
5. South: each person states a stance or a next step. A stance may be "not yet", tied to a north item.
6. Do not average the bearings into a fake consensus. Report the spread.

**Source.** Harvard Project Zero, Compass Points. [Visible thinking routines](https://pz.harvard.edu/thinking-routines).
