# Situation–behavior–impact

**Job.** Give feedback as a situation, an observable behavior, and the impact, without a motive.

**Not this.** A performance case built from a mind-reading ("you don't care"). If you cannot point at a behavior a camera would catch, you are not ready to use this page. Also not a systems diagnosis of why the behavior repeats.

**Diagram.** Three sentences. Situation. Behavior. Impact. Nothing else in the core.

**Steps.**
1. Situation: when and where, specific enough that the person knows which moment.
2. Behavior: what they did or said. Verbs a witness could confirm. Remove adjectives about character.
3. Impact: the effect on the work, the customer, or you. Speak for the effect you actually know. Do not narrate their intent.
4. Stop and let them respond. The tool is the three sentences plus the pause.
5. If a request follows, make it a future behavior, not a trait.

**Source.** Situation–behavior–impact feedback. Worksheet form: [Untools](https://untools.co). The Center for Creative Leadership is the usual source of the form.
