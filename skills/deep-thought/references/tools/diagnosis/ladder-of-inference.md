# Ladder of Inference

**Job.** Catch the jump from observable data to a selected story, a belief, and an action.

**Not this.** The structure of the system is the question (iceberg, loops). This page is one mind's leap, not the system's shape.

**Diagram.** A ladder: data, selected data, meaning, assumptions, conclusion, belief, action.

**Steps.**
1. Write the action or conclusion someone is about to treat as fact.
2. Drop to the data. What was seen or heard, in words a camera could confirm?
3. Mark what was selected and what was ignored.
4. Name the assumption that turned the selection into a belief.
5. Write one other meaning the same data can hold.
6. Act on the conclusion, or go get the rung that is missing. Do not skip the rung and keep the action.

**Source.** Chris Argyris; used in Peter Senge, *The Fifth Discipline*. Worksheet form: [Untools](https://untools.co).
