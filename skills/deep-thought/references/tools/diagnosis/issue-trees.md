# Issue trees

**Job.** Split one question into branches that do not overlap and that together cover it, until the leaves are answerable.

**Not this.** You are categorizing causes of a defect (Ishikawa) or drawing influence between actors (connection circles). A tree that smuggles a solution into the root is not a question.

**Diagram.** A tree. One question at the root. Leaves you could settle with a fact or a decision.

**Steps.**
1. Write the root as one question.
2. Split it into branches a newcomer could tell apart.
3. Test the split. Can one item sit in two branches? Does any live case fall outside all of them? Repair the split before going deeper.
4. Repeat until each leaf is a fact you can get or a decision you can make.
5. Mark the leaves that would change the root answer. Work those. Leave the rest.

**Source.** Issue-tree practice in the lineage of Barbara Minto. Worksheet form: [Untools](https://untools.co).
