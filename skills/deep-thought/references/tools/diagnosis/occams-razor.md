# Occam's razor

**Job.** Prefer the explanation that adds the fewest new assumptions and still fits the facts you have.

**Not this.** The simpler story drops a fact or a mechanism you have already observed. Few assumptions do not outrank contradictory evidence. Also not a rebuild from physics (first principles).

**Diagram.** Competing explanations, each with its unsupported assumptions counted.

**Steps.**
1. List the explanations actually on the table. Do not add one to make a set.
2. Under each, list assumptions that are not yet evidenced.
3. Remove any explanation that contradicts an observed fact, however short it is.
4. Prefer the survivor with the fewest remaining new assumptions.
5. Name the one observation that would force the more complicated survivor back into contention.

**Source.** William of Ockham. Orientation: [Farnam Street, mental models](https://fs.blog/mental-models/).
