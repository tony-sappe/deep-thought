# Two lists

**Job.** Make a short focus list and treat the rest as avoid-at-all-costs until the focus list is done.

**Not this.** A ranking of everything you still intend to do. The second list is not a backlog. It is a refusal. Also not Eisenhower, which still allows a scheduled quadrant.

**Diagram.** Two lists. Five or fewer items on the focus list. Every other live goal on the avoid list.

**Steps.**
1. Write the goals that are genuinely live. Cap the writing at twenty-five. If you have more, you are inventorying wishes.
2. Circle at most five. Those are the focus list. Choose them. Do not circle by voting in a way that leaves twelve "top" items.
3. Move every uncircled goal to the avoid list.
4. The avoid list is not "later" under another name. Until a focus item is finished or explicitly killed, do not spend the scarce resource on an avoid item.
5. When a focus item completes, repeat. Do not backfill from the avoid list by drift.

**Source.** Commitment-device story widely attributed to Warren Buffett via Mike Flint. Treat it as a device, not as a letter you can cite line by line. Indexed by ModelThinkers.
