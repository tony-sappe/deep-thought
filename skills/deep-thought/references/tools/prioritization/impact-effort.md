# Impact–effort

**Job.** Sort candidates by the value of doing them versus the cost of doing them.

**Not this.** Urgency versus importance (Eisenhower). A cheap item can be urgent and still be low impact. Do not smuggle "strategic" in as a third secret axis.

**Diagram.** Two axes. Impact vertical, effort horizontal. Four regions: do first (high impact, low effort), plan (high impact, high effort), fill only if free (low impact, low effort), avoid (low impact, high effort).

**Steps.**
1. List candidates that are real pieces of work, not themes.
2. Score impact as consequence if it lands, effort as cost to land it. Use the same rough scale for every item. Do not pretend the scale is data.
3. Place them. Break ties by asking which high-impact item's effort estimate is the least fictional.
4. Do a high-impact, low-effort item next, or explicitly choose a high-impact, high-effort item and say what you are delaying for it.
5. Name at least one avoid. If everything is high impact, the impact axis is not doing any work. Rescale.

**Source.** No single originator. This page is the common sort of value against the cost of doing the work. [Untools](https://untools.co) is a worksheet, not the source.
