# Circle of competence

**Job.** Gate the decision on whether your judgment is reliable here, before you apply a finer tool.

**Not this.** A technique for stretching into a domain you do not understand. The circle is a boundary. Studying for an afternoon does not move it. Use it before a matrix, a model, or a bet.

**Diagram.** Three zones for this specific decision: inside, at the edge, outside.

**Steps.**
1. State the judgment the decision requires (a market, a technology, a person's reliability, a regulatory reading).
2. Inside: you have a track record of predictions here, and you know what would falsify them. Proceed to the decision tool.
3. Edge: you understand the vocabulary but your record is thin. Name the person or the probe that covers the gap. Do not average your confidence upward.
4. Outside: you cannot tell a real expert from a confident one. Decline the bet, buy the competence, or shrink the decision until it sits inside.
5. Write the boundary in one sentence so a later you cannot quietly expand it.

**Source.** Warren Buffett and Charlie Munger, circle of competence. Orientation: [Farnam Street](https://fs.blog/mental-models/).
